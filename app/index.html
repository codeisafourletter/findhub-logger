What can I import?

JSON array or { records: [...] }, or CSV with captured_at, person, latitude, longitude headers. Optional: address, battery, displayed_age. Timestamps need a timezone, for example 2026-11-04T12:30:00Z. Export your Google Sheet as CSV. Imports replace the current session only after every row validates.

Tracked entities
